# Security policy

PureMac works with your files, so we take its safety seriously. Thank you for helping keep it and
the people who use it safe.

## Please report privately

**Don't open a public issue for a security problem.** Report it privately instead, using either:

- **GitHub:** [Report a vulnerability](https://github.com/OpenAppBox/PureMac/security/advisories/new)
  (this repository's *Security* tab), or
- **Email:** [support@openappbox.com](mailto:support@openappbox.com) with "PureMac security" in the
  subject line.

Helpful to include:

- What you found and why it matters
- Steps to reproduce it, ideally with a small example folder layout
- The PureMac version and your macOS version
- Whether the problem is already known publicly

## What we consider a security problem

For example:

- PureMac moving, changing or deleting anything it shouldn't — outside the items you reviewed, in a
  protected location, or something other than what it showed you
- A way to make PureMac follow a link or swap into a place it should never touch
- Anything that sends data off your Mac
- Anything that gains more rights than the user running PureMac

A wrong size, a missed cache or a confusing message is a regular bug — please
[open an issue](https://github.com/OpenAppBox/PureMac/issues/new/choose) for those.

## What happens next

1. We confirm we've received your report, usually within a few days.
2. We investigate and keep you updated on what we find.
3. We fix it, release an update, and publish an advisory. With your permission, we credit you.

Please give us a reasonable time to release a fix before sharing details publicly.

## Supported versions

Security fixes are made for the **latest release** of PureMac. Please update before reporting, if
you can.
