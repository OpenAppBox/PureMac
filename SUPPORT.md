# Getting help with PureMac

## Something isn't working

[Open an issue](https://github.com/OpenAppBox/PureMac/issues/new/choose) so everyone can follow the
fix. Helpful to include:

- Your PureMac version (**PureMac → About PureMac**) and macOS version
- What you did, what you expected, and what happened
- Screenshots, if you have them — please hide any file names you'd rather not share

## A question or an idea

- **Ideas and feature requests:** [open an issue](https://github.com/OpenAppBox/PureMac/issues/new/choose)
  and tell us what you're trying to get done.
- **Questions about using PureMac:** see the [README](README.md#questions),
  [How PureMac keeps your files safe](docs/safety.md) and [openappbox.com/puremac](https://www.openappbox.com/puremac).
- **Prefer email?** Write to [support@openappbox.com](mailto:support@openappbox.com). Every message is
  read by the people who build PureMac.

## Subscriptions and billing

Subscriptions, cancellations and refunds are handled by the store you bought from. You can manage
or cancel them anytime in your store account. For the Mac App Store, refunds can be requested at
[reportaproblem.apple.com](https://reportaproblem.apple.com).

## Security problems

Please report them privately — see [SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md).

## Common answers

**PureMac lists some locations as "not readable".**
macOS protects some folders, such as Mail and Safari. Allow PureMac under **System Settings → Privacy
& Security → Full Disk Access** to measure them too. It's optional.

**Free space didn't go up after a cleanup.**
Items in the Trash still use space until you empty it. PureMac never empties the Trash for you.

**An app's caches were "held back".**
The app is running. Quit it (in Smart Clean, *Show* brings it to the front) and PureMac includes them by itself.

**I want something back.**
Open **History** in PureMac and choose *Put Back*, or open the Trash in Finder and choose
**File → Put Back**.
